The marks

Four ways to sign a piece. One emblem in three colorways, and the sliced wordmark.

The mark is a math problem that never repeats.

The emblem is a Lorenz attractor, the first picture of chaos theory. Two paths that start almost on top of each other end up nowhere near each other. That is the brand: small decisions, loud results.
